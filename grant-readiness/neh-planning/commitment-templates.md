# Commitment templates and factual intake

**Unsent working templates. These are not letters of support, institutional agreements, résumés or evidence of appointments.** Replace bracketed fields only with verified information, have each signer review their own commitments, and obtain signed, dated originals. Do not upload these blanks as letters.pdf.

## Applicant institution: facts and authorization

Legal name / eligibility evidence / UEI / active SAM registration / authorized Grants.gov representative: [complete]. Mission, operating budget, actual comparable attendance, staff availability and relevant collection/venue: [document]. Named project director and grant administrator: [confirm distinct responsibilities]. Decision-making, rights control, record stewardship, public access and financial accountability: [specify]. Relationship with external director or researcher: [written agreement]. Proposed budget and indirect/fringe treatment: [approve]. Institution’s own substantive exhibition purpose: [explain].

## Researcher or consultant scope

Rate or fixed fee and maximum amount: [agreed]. Invoice and approval process: [complete]. Reproduction rights and licenses: [complete]. Conflicts/procurement classification: [institutional determination]. No guarantee of payment exists without an executed agreement and available funds.

## Rights request checklist

Source title and creator; custodian; exact image/text/version; proposed display, online and print uses; term/territory; credit; fee; restrictions; authorized response. Do not infer permission from possession of a scan or a website footer. Retain correspondence and the permission itself.

## Résumé intake

For each actual team member: full name/title/affiliation, relevant training, selected publications/projects, role and availability. Obtain their reviewed résumé. Suggested NEH length is two pages per person; no credentials were generated for this packet.
