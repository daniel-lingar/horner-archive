# Work plan: Exhibitions Planning

**Draft period: 1 November 2027–31 October 2028. All roles unappointed; applicant must confirm.** These are proposed award-period activities, not a restart of work already completed. The NOFO start window begins 1 November 2027; the program page lists 1 October. Use the NOFO for this draft and resolve the discrepancy with NEH before filing.

| Period | Activity and dependency | Responsible role | Reviewable output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months 1–2 | Confirm interpretive questions; select resources from v21 catalog; scholars review regional context | Institutional director; two outside historians; researcher | Approved interpretive brief and selected-source register |
| Months 2–4 | Verify selected originals, transcriptions and rights; resolve only questions needed for labels | Researcher; historians; institutional rights lead | Object/source list with citations, permissions status and explicit exclusions |
| Months 4–6 | Draft four-section visitor sequence; prototype labels and image alternatives | Designer; historians; accessibility reviewer | Design concept, sample labels, access review |
| Months 7–8 | Test with two small adult groups after host/recruitment agreement | Evaluation consultant; host; coordinator | Anonymized feedback, counts and interpretation findings |
| Months 9–10 | Revise content and design from test; companion educator review | Historians; designer; education consultant | Revised concept, corrected labels and companion sheets |
| Months 11–12 | Define implementation scope, production costs, rights remaining and stewardship | Institutional director; coordinator; designer | Final planning report, costed implementation plan and maintenance responsibilities |

The institutional director approves each stage before the next uses it. Unresolved source or permission problems lead to an attributed alternative or exclusion, not an unsupported label. No final exhibition fabrication or opening is claimed within this planning request.
