# Errata | 19 October 2026

This page records corrections established by the Phase 6 evidence recovery (19 October 2026) against the superseded v20 edition and the v21 reading edition. It is organized in three parts: **(A) newly verified historical facts** the archive now asserts on primary-source grounds; **(B) legacy claims withdrawn or downgraded** because their stated evidence was never produced; **(C) unresolved items** carried forward. Earlier editions are preserved with their dated correction notices; nothing here is silently overwritten. Superseded wording remains recoverable in `superseded-v20.html` and Git history.

## Part A — Newly verified historical facts

**E1. William Riley Horner's service and death — VERIFIED against a contemporary official source.** The 1867 *Report of the Adjutant General of Arkansas* (GPO, 1867; IA `reportadjutantg01offigoog`), 2nd Arkansas Infantry roster, Company K, "Died" section, printed p. 231: "Horner, William R." — Private. Enlisted Dec. 6, 1863. Mustered Feb. 19, 1864. Remarks: "Killed by guerillas in Johnson county, Arkansas, August 10, 1864." This is the archive's first primary-source verification of its central service/death claim. It does not verify the circumstances beyond the roster's six words, the head-wound account (later family testimony), or William's duty status on August 10.

**E2. Pleasant Horner's death — CORROBORATED; the "August 12" variant withdrawn.** The same roster (p. 231): "Horner, Pleasant" — Private. Enlisted Mar. 29, 1864. Mustered June 27, 1864. Remarks: "Died of fever at Lewisburg, Arkansas, August 4, 1864." The superseded edition's "died Aug 12 per military record" (v20.html:492) is withdrawn — no such record has been produced. Attribution of this roster entry to the younger Pleasant (b. ~1845) is inference from enlistment age; the roster gives no age.

**E3. Andrew Horner's death — probable Andrew Jackson match.** The same roster (p. 231): "Horner, Andrew" — Private. Enlisted Mar. 29, 1864. Mustered June 27, 1864. Remarks: "Died of measles at Lewisburg, Arkansas, July 31, 1864." The roster prints no middle initial; identity as Andrew Jackson Horner is probable but unconfirmed.

**E4. The OAR transcription is corrected.** In the *Official Army Register of the Volunteer Force*, Part 4, 2nd Arkansas Infantry (printed p. 1172): the resigned 1st lieutenant's name is printed "Harrington" ("Harriogton" was an OCR artifact, not a printed sic); the Major's date reads 25 Feb., 1864 per the in-volume errata (not 20 May, 1864).

**E5. Phase 5's OAR findings are corrected.** (a) The OAR's Arkansas Infantry section comprises four organizations — 1st Regiment (p. 1170), 1st Battalion (p. 1171), 2nd Regiment (p. 1172), 4th Regiment (p. 1173) — not just the 2nd and 4th. (b) The 1st Battalion (Helena, July 1862, 6-months men, mustered out Dec 31, 1862) existed through fall 1862; the corrected claim is that no *2nd Arkansas* organization existed in fall 1862. This does not rescue the Goodspeed service narrative, which names the 2nd Arkansas and supplies a Co. K / 2nd-lieutenant / February-1864-resignation sequence the battalion cannot provide.

**E6. August 2 vs. August 10 — linkage completed.** The v21 correction (C23) stands: William Riley's tombstone and the official roster give his death as August 10, 1864; those sources do not independently establish an August 2 attack. The superseded edition's linkage is corrected by citation to C23.

## Part B — Legacy claims withdrawn or downgraded

**F1 — William Riley muster-roll quotation (attribution withdrawn; substance verified).** The superseded edition quoted "reported killed by guerillas 10 August, 1864, Johnson Co., Arkansas" (:509), attributed it to "NARA M399" with "Confirms:" (:869), designated "William Riley Horner CMSR, Co. K" as tier Primary, "Reviewed (Fold3)" (:980), and attributed "reported killed by guerrillas 10 August, 1864" to "Muster roll, August 8, 1865" (:1002). No card image, Fold3 capture, or NARA order number appears in any edition; exact-phrase web search (both spellings) returns zero hits outside the archive. The attributions and designations are withdrawn. The *substance* of the claim is now verified per E1 above — the archive asserts the service and death on the roster's authority, not on the unproduced quotation. *Status: established overstatement corrected; substance verified.*

**F2 — "Confirmed" 27-day militia muster (downgraded).** The superseded edition called the 27-day muster as 1st Lieutenant, Co. I, 10th Arkansas Militia (22 Feb–19 Mar 1862, Clarksville, under Capt. Christopher C. Casey) "confirmed" (:596) while elsewhere noting the rolls "may contain" the enrollment (:770). No muster roll has been produced. *Corrected wording:* "Family and legacy accounts report a 27-day muster…; no muster roll has been produced. The Arkansas State Archives militia rolls are the outstanding source." *Status: established overstatement.*

**F3 — Pension rejection grounds stated as fact (withdrawn as stated).** The superseded edition stated that the War Department ruled in 1894 that Horner "enrolled men without formally enlisting himself" (:602, :770) and designated the unretrieved application 1,143,763 as tier Primary (:985), while its own filing date is inconsistent — "(1893)" (:899) vs. "Filed March 2, 1894. Rejected October 5, 1894" (:930). The file has never been retrieved. *Corrected wording:* "The archive reports — without the file — that Application 1,143,763 was rejected on the ground that Horner recruited men but never formally enlisted. The application file has not been inspected; the filing date is inconsistently given as 1893 and March 2, 1894. Tier-Primary designations attached to this unretrieved file are withdrawn." The 1894 rejection is not evidence of non-service; it is an unverified report of an uninspected file. *Status: established overstatement.*

**F4 — Pleasant Horner death-date inconsistency (resolved).** See E2. *Status: established error corrected.*

**F5 — August 2 fixed by the tombstone (linkage).** See E6. *Status: established error (already corrected in v21 as C23; linkage completed here).*

**F6 — Goodspeed reliance (scope-finalized).** Three legs: (1) the 1888 sketch is family-furnished (print-verified); (2) its service narrative is chronologically impossible as written (no 2nd Arkansas organization existed in fall 1862; the regiment's Camden engagement postdates the alleged February 1864 resignation); (3) the OAR Part 4 officer lists for all four Arkansas infantry organizations plus the volume index contain no Horner. The OAR scope caveat is now final: the register's roster is "as it stood on the day of muster out" with casualty lists; its front matter never claims the Resigned list is comprehensive; absence there closes the commission claim as far as the Army's register goes without proving non-service. *Status: established overstatement; correction stands with the scope qualification.*

**F7 — Hiram Boen "CMSR (M399, 13 cards)" (withdrawn).** The superseded edition claimed the cards "place him in Johnson or Newton County for the entire August 1864 murder period" (:565). No cards produced; AWOL ≠ location (already corrected in v21 as C17). The "13 cards" specificity is withdrawn. *Status: established overstatement (already corrected in v21).*

**F8 — Fahnstrom "two brother in laws" lead (demoted, retained as contradicting tradition).** The detail sat unnoted in a cited source (OQ-12). The Fahnstrom 2000 page reports "Two of his brother in laws, died within two weeks" in the Francis Marion Acord narrative (d. 28 Apr 1864, per the same page). The page names neither man; the temporal anchor is ambiguous; the page is derivative family history with internal inconsistencies. The AG roster's July 31 / August 4 entries for Andrew and Pleasant Horner substantially supersede any April/May implication. *Status: omission corrected; lead retained with its demotion documented.*

**F9 — OAR "Part 8" retrieval plan (corrected).** A prior phase's research note placed Arkansas regiments in Part 8, "not on Internet Archive" — wrong on both counts (Part 4; on IA as `officialarmyreg00offigoog`). A prior-phase research error, not archive content; the OAR retrieval plan targets Part 4. *Status: established error in prior-phase notes.*

**F10 — OQ-14 "confirmed" (downgraded).** The superseded edition labeled the George Washington Middleton finding "confirmed" (:788) while admitting CMSR retrieval "would confirm the card text." "Confirmed" meant consistency-checked, not record-inspected. *Corrected wording:* "Reported (consistent with regimental history); CMSR not inspected." *Status: established overstatement of a low-stakes item.*

**F11 — Patent sections (corrected).** The v21 reading edition states "160 acres in sections 19 and 22" (:15). The patent image (Certificate 19B) shows the legal description covering three sections: 19, 20, and 22. *Corrected wording:* "…in sections 19, 20, and 22, Township 12 North, Range 24 West…" *Status: established error carried into v21.*

## Part C — Unresolved items carried forward (not errors; open questions)

**N1.** v20.html:899 asserts "Permelia Turner Horner filed widow's pension August 17, 1865" and "Dr. John Turner Horner filed minor pension for orphaned Acord children September 7, 1865." Specific dates, no index entries produced — UNVERIFIED. The Acord-guardianship assertion conflicts with the Fahnstrom page (guardian: brother Calvin Acord). Both need pension-index verification before any use. New target: the minor's pension file for Francis Marion Acord's sons (NARA RG 15).

**N2.** The 4th Infantry's Andrew/Pleasant Horner desertion entries (AG roster p. 234) vs. the 2nd Infantry's death entries (p. 231) — same men vs. different men UNRESOLVED; see the identity register. Do not merge.

**N3.** Lewis C. Horner, Co. K, 2nd Arkansas (enl. Feb. 15, 1864, mustered out with regiment, AG roster p. 230) — new ancillary Horner identity; relationship to the family unknown.

**N4.** v20.html :397/:399 apply the August 2, 1864 date to Spencer ("MURDERED August 2, 1864") — review whether F5's correction propagates there. The August 2 attack date and Spencer's death date remain working dates with no located source.
