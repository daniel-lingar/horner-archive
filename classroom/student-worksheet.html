Student worksheet: classify and explain

D: directly supported means the record explicitly states it. I: reasonable inference means a conclusion follows from evidence with a stated assumption or limit. N: not supported by these sources means the evidence supplied does not establish the claim; that does not necessarily mean it is false.

Write 100–150 words. Include two accurate statements, references [A] and [B], one clear limit, and a specific next record with the question it might answer. You may include an inference if you label its assumption. You do not need to decode uncertain handwriting or discuss killings.
