# Source display review gate

Before an approved source release, a named human reviewer must open the actual image and compare its heading, people, dates and relevant passage with the caption, transcription and proposed claim. Confirm provenance, identifiers, reproduction rights and any derivative's exact crop. Record reviewer/date/outcome; a model-assisted visual check is not a human sign-off. Do not fill unknown names or difficult words by inference. Keep source images unaltered and describe derivatives.

Public review drafts must retain the pending-review label and must not imply human approval.

The v21.2 census/patent crops were visually compared by the editing assistant on 17 October 2026. **Independent human review is pending**; the lesson is a review draft, not an approved classroom release. Uncertain entries are excluded from scored items. An assignee's ancestry, exact modern location and disputed tract readings are not inferred. A release check must distinguish technical checks from source authentication and classroom piloting.
