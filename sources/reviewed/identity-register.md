# Identity register | proposed 19 October 2026

This register records identities that must not be merged. A shared surname — or a shared given name — is not an identification. Entries are resolved only by independent evidence (CMSR jacket comparison, pension file, or contemporary civil record), never by name resemblance.

## The two hypotheses (both live — do not choose)

**(a) Same men, dual records.** The 4th and 2nd organized simultaneously in early 1864, and the 4th was consolidated into the 2nd on October 28, 1864 (per the OAR). Both date sequences are chronologically compatible with men carried on both regiments' rolls: e.g., Pleasant enlists in the 2nd (Mar. 29), signs with the 4th (Apr. 27, never mustered), is marked deserted from the 4th (May 5), then musters into the 2nd (June 27). Inter-regimental movement and desertion-reenlistment were common; the 2nd had a documented Lewisburg desertion wave and a formal restoration order (31 Dec 1864). Under this reading the "desertion" marks may reflect paperwork rather than actual absence.

**(b) Different men.** Andrew Horner and Pleasant Horner are not unique names; two pairs in Arkansas Union service simultaneously is possible. The conflicting enlistment particulars (different enlistment and muster dates) are what a different-men reading predicts.

**What would resolve it:** the M399 CMSR jackets for the 2nd's and 4th's Andrew and Pleasant Horner, compared jacket-to-jacket on age, birthplace, occupation, and physical description. The AG roster supplies none of these fields. No other record in hand distinguishes the hypotheses.
