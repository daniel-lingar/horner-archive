# NARA Original-Record Acquisition Plan | 19 October 2026

Documentation only — prepared, not sent. No orders submitted, no fees incurred. Re-verify the current form revision and fee at order time.

**Procedures verified 19 October 2026** from the current NATF Form 85 and NATF Form 86 (rev. 10-2019, OMB Control No. 3095-0027), as published in the September 2026 OMB information-collection documents. Form revisions and fees change; re-verify at order time.

**How to order (both forms):**
- **Online (fastest):** eservices.archives.gov/orderonline — account required; email confirmation; order trackable at no extra cost.
- **Mail:** Archival Operations Washington D.C., Form 85 – Pension *or* Form 86 – Military, National Archives and Records Administration, 700 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20408-0001.
- **Payment:** credit card only — MasterCard, VISA, American Express, Discover (card validation code required). **Do NOT send cash or check.** Forms with incomplete card information are destroyed, not held.
- **Timing:** allow up to 90 days for processing.
- **Unsuccessful searches:** no charge. **Refunds:** essentially none — NARA does not refund legible reproductions; errors are corrected or refunded only if NARA verifies it processed the order incorrectly (contact within 30 days of delivery).
- **Delivery:** paper copies, CD/DVD, or electronic transfer (download link by email; files available 30 days).
- **Alternative access (no NARA order):** a Fold3 subscription covers the M399 CMSR microfilm and much of the pension numerical index — faster than mail for the service records, at the subscription's own cost. This is an access method, not an evidence claim.

**Cost:** up to **$270** if all 9 jackets are located (9 × $30); **$0** if none are found. **Recommended sequencing to control cost:** order A1–A5 first (William R., both Andrews, both Pleasants — $150 maximum) since these test F1's exact wording, the duty-status question, and the identity conflict; then A6–A9.

**What to expect from a CMSR jacket:** compiled cards abstracting muster rolls, which may include enlistment/muster dates, physical description, age, birthplace, occupation, and remarks (including desertion and death notations) — no single card is guaranteed to carry all of these fields. They do not contain family correspondence or narrative accounts. If the card text differs from the archive's withdrawn quotation, the card controls.

- **Form:** NATF Form 85, check **"Full Pension Application File — Civil War, 1860 and Later."**
- **Fee: $80 for the first 100 pages + $0.70 per additional page.** (Pension Documents Packet $30 and pre-Civil War $55 do not apply here; bounty-land $30 does not apply.)
- **Blocks 2–6 (required):** Veteran: Horner, John Turner. Branch: Army. State: Arkansas. War: Civil War, Union, Volunteer. Application/file no.: **1,143,763** (invalid application).
- **Additional blocks:** Unit: 2nd Arkansas Infantry (claimed). Date of death: June 20, 1911. Place of death: Cassville, Barry County, Missouri. In Comments, note: "Archive reports filing as both 1893 and March 2, 1894 — date unconfirmed; requesting file to verify filing date, service claim, and rejection grounds."

Civil War pension files are often large; if the file exceeds the page limit NARA sends the first block with a quote for the remainder — the requester must return the quote with payment to receive the rest.

## Package C — William Riley Horner widow's pension WC75885 (NATF Form 85)

- **Form:** NATF Form 85.
- **Recommended first pass:** check **"Pension Documents Packet" — $30.** The packet contains, to the extent present in the file, up to 8 genealogical documents: declaration of pension, declaration of widow's pension, Adjutant General statements of service, applicant questionnaires, "Pension Dropped" cards, marriage certificates, death certificates, discharge certificate. NARA will not provide a page count of the remainder; if the full file is then wanted, order the complete file separately ($80 + $0.70/page over 100).
- **Blocks 2–6 (required):** Veteran: Horner, William Riley. Branch: Army. State: Arkansas. War: Civil War, Union, Volunteer. Pension/certificate no.: **WC75885** (widow's certificate). Unit: 2nd Arkansas Infantry, Co. K.
- **Block 17 (widow/claimant):** Mary Ann Boen Horner (widow). Date of veteran's death: August 10, 1864. Place: Johnson County, Arkansas.
- **What to expect:** the widow's declaration, proof of marriage, the Adjutant General's statement of William's service (an independent check on the roster), and any death statements or witness affidavits — the material the archive's summary drew on, now verifiable in full context. Note: the Sturges account, if present anywhere in the file, can then be dated and placed — it is currently a descendant account of unverified provenance, not a contemporary witness statement.

## Ordering checklist (all packages)

1. Re-verify the current form revision and fee at archives.gov or eservices.archives.gov/orderonline before ordering — fees and forms change.
2. One form per file (Form 85) and per man per organization (Form 86). Do not batch multiple veterans on one form.
3. Complete all required blocks — NARA destroys forms without return addresses and will not process incomplete credit-card information.
4. Keep a copy of each completed form; record the order number from NARA's email confirmation.
5. Do not request certified copies on these forms (NARA cannot digitally certify; contact NARA separately if certification is ever needed).
